Research Assistant — Neuromorphic Hardware Co-Design

San Diego State University · Nov 2025 – Present

Designed and sized a transistor-level CMOS leaky integrate-and-fire neuron implementing membrane integration, leakage, threshold detection, spike generation, and reset — then carried it through custom layout and back into a system-level model.

Analog circuit design→ Transistor sizing→ Simulation→ Custom layout→ System-level calibration
  • Circuit design. Functional decomposition of the neuron, current-mirror design and bias-current generation, MOS sizing and operating-point optimisation against firing-threshold, spike-frequency, power, and area trade-offs.
  • Circuit behaviour. Membrane-node capacitance and leakage behaviour, threshold detection, and reset circuitry.
  • Simulation. Transient and DC analysis in Spectre; F–I response characterisation, membrane time-constant extraction, and spike waveform analysis.
  • Layout. Parasitic-aware custom layout preserving device matching, with defensive routing on the charge-sensitive membrane node.
  • Technology. First implementation in the NCSU 0.35 µm CDK; currently re-sizing and rebuilding the design in GPDK45, where multiplier-based device fingering is available.
  • System level. Ported measured circuit parameters into a Python/SpikingJelly LIF model and quantified the cost of silicon non-idealities on MNIST: 94.5% circuit-calibrated vs. 97.4% ideal.
