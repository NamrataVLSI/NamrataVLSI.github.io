Custom layout

6T SRAM Bitcell & Array — Custom Layout

Cross-coupled 6T bitcell with shared rails, differential bit-lines, and array-level layout in SAED 28/32 nm

Synopsys Custom Compiler · IC Validator · StarRC · SAED 28/32 nm

Role in the project

Sole author: bitcell schematic and sizing, full-custom bitcell layout, array assembly, physical verification, parasitic extraction, and pre-/post-layout correlation.

SRAM timing, static noise margin, and power figures are not claimed here — those characterisation results are not part of the documented result set.

Circuit structure

  • Cross-coupled inverter pair forming the bistable storage element.
  • Access transistors connecting the storage nodes to the differential bit-lines, gated by the word line.
  • Device ratios — the relative strength of pull-down, access, and pull-up devices governs whether the cell can be read without disturbing its state and written when required.

Layout and array organisation

  • Symmetry between the two storage halves so both states behave identically.
  • Shared supply rails across abutting cells, with mirrored tiling to share contacts and diffusion at cell boundaries.
  • Word-line distribution across the row and differential bit-lines down the column.
  • Matching and parasitic considerations — bit-line capacitance grows with array height and directly affects read behaviour, so column routing was kept tight and balanced between BL and BLB.

Verification flow

Bitcell layout→DRC→LVS→Array tiling→Array DRC / LVS / ERC→PEX→Pre-/post-layout correlation

Key learnings

  • The bitcell is a tiling problem: what happens at the boundary matters as much as what happens inside.
  • Bit-line parasitics are an array-level property that a single-cell view never exposes.
  • Cell symmetry has to survive contact and via placement, not just device placement.
