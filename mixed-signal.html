Mixed-signal

Mixed-Signal IC Design — PLL & SerDes

Charge-pump phase-locked loop and an 8:1 serializer / 1:8 deserializer in GPDK45, from block architecture through layout and verification

Cadence Virtuoso · Spectre · GPDK45

Role in the project

Sole author: block-level architecture, transistor-level schematic design, testbench development, pre-layout simulation, layout, and verification, working bottom-up from individual blocks to top-level integration.

Scope: this page covers the two blocks that are built — the charge-pump PLL and the serializer/deserializer pair. A SAR ADC and a DAC are planned for the same repository but are not implemented yet, and nothing is claimed for them.

Phase-locked loop

A charge-pump PLL decomposed into its constituent blocks, each designed and simulated independently before hierarchical integration.

PFD→Charge pump→Loop filter→VCO→Divider→Clock buffer→Top level
  • Phase-frequency detector — produces up/down pulses proportional to the phase error between reference and feedback clocks.
  • Charge pump — converts those pulses into charge delivered to the loop filter; current matching between up and down paths determines static phase offset.
  • Loop filter — integrates charge into the VCO control voltage and shapes the loop dynamics.
  • Voltage-controlled oscillator — control voltage sets output frequency; the tuning characteristic has to cover the target range with usable linearity.
  • Frequency divider — closes the loop and sets the multiplication ratio.
  • Clock buffer — drives the distributed clock load without degrading edge quality.
  • Top-level integration — hierarchical assembly and transient verification of lock behaviour.

Serializer and deserializer

An 8:1 serializer and a 1:8 deserializer built as a matched pair, converting between an eight-bit parallel word and a single serial stream at eight times the word rate.

  • Parallel-to-serial conversion — the eight-bit word is loaded in parallel and shifted out one bit per serial clock cycle.
  • Serial-to-parallel conversion — incoming bits are shifted in and presented as a parallel word once the full frame has arrived.
  • Clocking and timing — the serial clock runs at eight times the parallel word rate, so the load and shift phases have to be sequenced without overlap.
  • Functional verification — simulated across load and shift sequences to confirm bit ordering and frame alignment survive the round trip.
  • Hierarchical integration — assembled from custom sequential building blocks rather than behavioural models.

Planned extensions

A SAR ADC (sample-and-hold, comparator, capacitive DAC, SAR control logic) and a DAC are planned additions to this repository. They are not built yet, so nothing is claimed for them here.

Design flow

Architecture→Schematic→Testbench→Pre-layout sim→Layout→DRC / LVS→PEX→Post-layout

Key learnings

  • In a PLL the blocks are only meaningful together — a VCO that meets spec alone can still break lock.
  • Charge-pump current matching is a small design detail with a large system-level consequence.
  • Bottom-up block verification saves far more time than debugging at top level.
