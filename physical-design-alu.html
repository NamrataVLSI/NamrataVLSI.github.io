Physical Synthesis & Timing Closure of a 32-bit Pipelined ALU

RTL through synthesis, floorplanning, placement, CTS, routing, and post-route timing closure in SAED 28 nm

Synopsys Design Compiler · IC Compiler II · PrimeTime · TCL · Python · SAED 28 nm

Role in the project

Sole author: RTL-to-GDS flow execution, SDC constraint authoring, block-level physical implementation, static timing analysis and closure, and TCL/Python automation of the reporting flow.

Design flow

RTL→Synthesis→Floorplan→Placement→CTS→Routing→Post-route STA→Timing closure

Implementation

  • Synthesis in Design Compiler from synthesizable RTL against the SAED 28 nm library.
  • Floorplanning, placement, CTS, and routing in IC Compiler II.
  • SDC constraints authored for clock definition, input/output delays, and design rule limits.
  • PrimeTime STA on critical paths, with setup and hold analysis across the timing graph.

Timing closure

  • Drove WNS and TNS to closure across post-route timing runs.
  • Cleared clock-skew, setup, and hold violations.
  • Cleared transition, capacitance, and fanout design-rule violations.
  • Fixes applied through cell sizing, buffer insertion, and placement and routing optimisation rather than by relaxing constraints.
  • PPA analysis across timing, area, power, and cell count at each stage.

Automation

Timing, area, power, cell-count, and violation reporting were automated in TCL and Python so that every implementation iteration produced a comparable report set instead of hand-collected numbers.

Key learnings

  • Most timing problems are floorplan problems surfacing late.
  • Hold fixing after CTS is a different discipline from setup closure and needs its own pass.
  • Automated reporting is what makes iteration-to-iteration comparison meaningful.
